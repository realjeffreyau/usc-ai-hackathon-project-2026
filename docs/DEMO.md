# 90-second demo

Use the fictional audit that opens with `npm start`. Do not use a personal STARS report or a real WebReg session for judging.

1. **0–15 seconds:** Degree Progress. Point out the Demo data label and separate earned, in-progress, and remaining units.
2. **15–30 seconds:** Four-Year Plan. Open a course to inspect prerequisites. Explain that later terms are estimates, not confirmed offerings.
3. **30–45 seconds:** Return to Degree Progress and click Find courses on GE-B. Enter “Find me a GE-B with a highly rated professor, no classes before 10, and keep me at 16 units or less.” Show the editable settings and the reader label.
4. **45–60 seconds:** Explain the top course’s requirement, meeting time, and sample rating. Add it to the plan, then open Schedule.
5. **60–75 seconds:** Compare with myCourseBin, load the sample, and show the conflict. Click Swap section and show it clear on the calendar.
6. **75–90 seconds:** Copy section numbers. Explain that registration remains manual and audits stay in browser memory.

Optional extension fallback: load `extension/` in Chrome and use the toolbar’s Try demo action. It opens a fictional rehearsal page without USC credentials.
